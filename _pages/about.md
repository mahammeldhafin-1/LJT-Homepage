I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/) (2024–present), where I am advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://junxianhe.github.io/) in the HKUST NLP Group. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2024.

My research lies at the intersection of **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with particular interests in:

- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
